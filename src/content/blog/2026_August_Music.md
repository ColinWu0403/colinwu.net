### Tigers Jaw - S/T

[Tigers Jaw](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tigers_Jaw_(album)>) is the band's second studio album, released in 2008.

I knew about Tigers Jaw for a while (having listened to some of their newer stuff) before so I decided to finally listen to their self-titled album which is an iconic album in the emo revival era. I really like DIY/raw sound of the album, the lyrics are youthful yet quite nostalgic too.

My favorite tracks on the album are [The Sun](https://youtu.be/SwG1qxKP1To?si=2cW-u6QHhhS_EWfR), [Plane Vs. Tank Vs. Submarine](https://youtu.be/wFmC7GPLTEs?si=2eKKxZnLedQBowYV), [I Saw Water](https://youtu.be/0a-FpI--RSQ?si=yYMVgr4YvF1DGY1q), [Between Your Band and the Other Band](https://youtu.be/JYCVQT6przs?si=L_a-KjetuZxJI117), and [Arms Across America](https://youtu.be/6saB45F2tug?si=wrIPSXBBoBZeTSM8).
