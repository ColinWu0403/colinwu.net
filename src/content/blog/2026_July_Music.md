### Madeon - Victory

[Victory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victory_%28Madeon_album%29) is [Madeon\'s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madeon) 3rd studio album that released at the end of June this year. It's one of my most anticipated releases this year, especially since Madeon's previous album [Good Faith](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Faith_(Madeon_album)>) came out back in 2019.

This album sonically is grittier and more distorted than anything he's previously done before, but still has his iconic energetic dance & electronic charm. Tracks like [Hi!](https://youtu.be/HK578NtfM84?si=313Wu5wPPOk_VErX), [Super Platinum](https://youtu.be/pA3GE-rXAqs?si=qu063S2VL1TphWUr) and [Red Jacket](https://youtu.be/pA3GE-rXAqs?si=suiK9575LP3jllex) use real electric guitars, but the production focuses on using digital distortion effects and the drums are all fully digital (I think).

My favorite tracks on the album are [Car Crash Baby](https://youtu.be/gcqnrXC4l6E?si=FfhfFVKAwiXAi-pf), [Dancing On Your Grave](https://youtu.be/DMATF47VyQA?si=BPDflUeMAl0OzZkl), [Somebody Else](https://youtu.be/YzxKEne2nBU?si=4IC3veuvrhBKCV_F), and [Enjoy](https://youtu.be/R5sLAb-YJTA?si=RL03_3dkisE2HGd8). Also [Fire Away](https://youtu.be/goFj5sgXx9E?si=4Uru_E-s0YuU6E9-) featuring [Slayyyter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slayyyter) is an unexpected collab (but slaps).

### acloudyskye - In a while this will all be gone

[acloudyskye](https://rateyourmusic.com/artist/acloudyskye) is criminally underrated (doesn't even have a wikipedia article so I linked his profile on RYM). He's a producer, singer, and songwriter from New York.

I'd describe his sound as indie/post rock with electronic and noise-pop influence. He's fully independent (no record label, self releases everything) and even makes all the album art & music videos himself.

All of his albums are concept albums that follow a central story of the [3 characters](https://acloudyskye.com/lore/) (on the album cover) exploring a post-apocalyptic world. This is his 6th studio album, having just released an album back in 2025.

I'd say this album is more melancholic and vocal driven than his earlier works. His first 3 albums have a unique orchestral/post-rock & dubstep sound that appears less here. This album seems more folk and shoegaze influenced, but it can still be very experimental.

My favorite tracks include [Ila's omen](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpvV-IoKAMk), [Algae](https://youtu.be/nn-1GL0HHOs?si=RG9RaWefsfulmNyK), [To tell you the truth](https://youtu.be/MjyKzvNWXLw?si=LZmt9A-xw_lK1Dtq), [Surf/Ichor](https://youtu.be/gpvV-IoKAMk?si=yXhtxH2dKEv2Sr_B&t=1100), and [Ache](https://youtu.be/9yd05XmXe4k?si=UJzynqh22WeWtCWX). The standout track is definitely the 10 minute and a half long epic that is [Tomorrow/Fish storm](https://youtu.be/gpvV-IoKAMk?si=tvBvyRFUzShg2nQe&t=1749).
