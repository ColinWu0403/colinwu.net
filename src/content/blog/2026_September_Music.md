## Highlighted Albums

### beabadoobee - Pylon

Can't believe I'm doing one for the artist who can't sing (sorry, had to). Anyways, this is her 4th studio album and possibly already my favorite from her discography, idc about recency bias, it's so good.

I'm really glad Bea went back to making more alternative/grunge sounding music. With this album, she brings an absolutely stacked guest list in the rock scene which I'll cover below.

Some of the tracks feel like the heaviest beabadoobee has ever been while other songs are soft and dreamy.

The majority of the album was written by Bea herself with [Gianluca Buccellati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gianluca_Buccellati) and Shane Moran of [Title Fight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Title_Fight) (I also did a review of <a href="/blog/2026_June_Music" rel="noopener noreferrer">Hyperview by Title Fight</a> in June). I'm considering this album the unofficial 5th Title Fight album since the band is never coming back 😢

The title track and opener [Pylon](https://youtu.be/a4xF8u5ArK8?si=FT1EPtX0K2tOw86R) really does set the tone of the album. With the lead single [Sun Has Set](https://youtu.be/OkApy8LE8s8?si=zNepbFYrpUi5UPxC) following, both tracks have Shane's awesome guitar riffs.

The next track [Estranged](https://youtu.be/n9LdTq6yXbY?si=Q4_Z5v2e-ywDKlAZ) has some of my favorite lyrics, so catchy. [Switchblade](https://youtu.be/EVrjt4pLC6g?si=tLqBtR5jQmqsVMG7) features more of the fuzzy guitar work.

[Write Me A Letter](https://youtu.be/vjr9Madg_sI?si=dX4QEo26SpRg0HpQ) might genuinely be one her best tracks. It features Matty Healy and George Daniels from [The 1975](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_1975) as guest producers for the track giving the track a dreamy feel. Then [It's Alright](https://youtu.be/sunbSwub1rI?si=LDXjikaf5lS9xjUo) follows with an upbeat and groovy feel.

[Memories](https://youtu.be/JkB41jZtyrQ?si=_4dcMq7Jv36gJLjm), another single for the album, sounds very nostalgic and slightly melancholic. [Nothing to Prove](https://youtu.be/CJWrBiyIWfo?si=9QgyQqnxAdt-gQbJ) includes additional vocals from [Hayley Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayley_Williams) (the goat).

[Radio](https://youtu.be/74XO2YO8EFU?si=7OEpmDtax1jGE964) is a top contender for my fave on the album too, the chorus is so good. [Powerlines](https://youtu.be/WV7sgza2tpE?si=3vqUpSarv5ykYCvB) features a verse from [Brendan Yates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Yates) of [Turnstile](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turnstile_(band)>).

[Spark](https://youtu.be/YOD0ApLzQK8?si=j7GM_oLQdPJ89wLM) is the most tender song on the album. [Evan Stephens Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_Stephens_Hall) of [Pinegrove](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinegrove_(band)>) helped write the song (Bea has said Pinegrove is one of her favorite bands).

Finally, the closing track [Satellite](https://youtu.be/p173tNIsskw?si=SdG2yNVhZAUuMq7q) finishes off with guest vocals from [Chino Moreno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chino_Moreno) of [Deftones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deftones).
